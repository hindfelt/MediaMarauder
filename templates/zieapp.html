01 / Member experience

Designed around the jobs an active network actually needs to do.

01

Signal

An editorial feed for podcasts, articles, video, and network updates. Content can be categorized, pinned, and targeted without turning the product into another noisy social feed.

02

Events

Role-aware invitations with agenda, location, categories, and RSVP. Members see what is relevant to them and can respond without leaving the app.

03

Domain asks

Focused expertise requests let members rate their knowledge, add context, and surface where the network can help. Administrators can prioritize the most important asks.

04

Contribute

A structured path for company tips and candidate referrals, including notes and optional CV material. Good network intelligence lands in the right workflow.

05

Criteria

A concise, always-current reference for the investment thesis. Members can qualify an idea before submitting it instead of hunting through an old presentation.

06

One identity

Signed Telegram authentication inside the Mini App, email one-time codes in the browser, and session continuity across the experience—without exposing the system behind it.

SIEreporting

Reporting lives inside the same product. Members capture receipts, log project time, follow approval status, and resubmit rejected items. The design keeps routine administration fast on both mobile and desktop.

01 / CAPTURE

Upload the evidence

Multiple attachments, receipt details, and project context enter one guided flow.

02 / EXTRACT

Let AI do the typing

Receipt data is interpreted automatically, while the member stays in control of the final values.

03 / REVIEW

Approve with context

Staff review a purpose-built queue with clear project, member, status, and documentation context.

04 / EXPORT

Close the month

Approved time and expenses become a structured finance bundle instead of a manual scavenger hunt.

01

Role-aware administration

Content, events, expertise requests, contributions, members, projects, and reporting live in one protected control plane. Audience and role filters stay consistent from creation to delivery.

02

Reporting operations

Administrators assign members to projects, review submissions, return items with reasons, and assemble the monthly finance hand-off without manipulating parallel spreadsheets.

03

Automation through MCP

A remote, authenticated tool surface makes the same administrative capabilities available to trusted AI workflows. Forty-four narrow tools favor explicit operations over broad access.

04

Authentication at the edge

Telegram signed data serves the Mini App, email OTP serves the browser, and Cloudflare Access protects staff surfaces. Rate limits and session controls sit close to the request boundary.

05

Privacy and traceability

Member targeting is enforced by role and audience. EU-hosted database and object storage keep product data regionally placed, while audit logging records sensitive administrative actions.

06

One deployable system

The React member app, protected admin, Workers API, relational data, object storage, email delivery, and web push are designed as one product—not a collection of disconnected utilities.
