02 The thread

I like software that makes an idea touchable. Sometimes useful. Sometimes strange. Preferably both.

0x4d is hexadecimal for the letter M. It is also a convenient umbrella for experiments that do not belong in a single category.

Everything here is designed, built, and shipped by Mattias Hindfelt—often with AI collaborators, always with a bias toward working software.
